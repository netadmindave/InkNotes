    Write the symbol in the box the way you normally do — 3 samples each is plenty. Your samples are used when converting formulas, especially for symbols like ∞, π, ∑ and Greek letters. Train as many or as few as you like.

    Writing in InkNotes

    Pages: notes are Letter (8.5×11) or A4 pages — pick in ⋮. Writing flows onto the next page; ＋ Add page adds a blank one. PDF export keeps the page size.

    Fingers: with ☝️ Finger writes on (default on phones), one finger writes and two fingers scroll and pinch-zoom. With it off, one finger scrolls — unless 🔒 Lock is on, then only two fingers move the page.

    Handwriting: write anywhere on the page. Tap ⇄ Convert (or lasso some strokes first) to turn it into neat text or a formula; the switch decides: Aa Text, ∑ Math or Auto (spots formulas line by line). You can fix the result before inserting. Hold the S Pen side button to erase.

    Typing: tap the page with your finger. Aa Text / ∑ Math also switches what you type. Switching while you write splits off a new block at the cursor. In a Math block, Enter finishes the formula and goes back to text.

    Math syntax (AsciiMath, or LaTeX if it starts with a backslash):

    x^2 + y_1
    (a+b)/(c-d)
    sqrt(2) root(3)(x)
    int_0^oo e^(-x^2) dx
    sum_(n=1)^N 1/n^2
    lim_(x->0) sin x / x
    (del n)/(del T)
    alpha, Delta, lambda, pi
    a <= b, x != y, +-, xx, ~~
    [[a,b],[c,d]]
    vec v, hat x, bar z, dot x

    In text, wrap math in $…$, or just write it — things like E = mc^2 or x_1 + x_2 are spotted automatically.

    Lists: start a line with - , 1. , # (heading) or [ ] (checkbox). **bold** works too.

    ✨ Clean up fixes spacing, capitals, doubled words and spelling, and leaves math alone. Words it doesn't know can be added to your dictionary. In ink areas it smooths your strokes. Undo (↶) reverts it.

    S Pen: in a text block, write with the pen to type (Samsung's S Pen to text, or the handwriting mode of the Samsung Keyboard). In an Ink area, draw freely; hold the pen's side button to erase.
