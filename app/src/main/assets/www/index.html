    Writing in InkNotes

    Aa Text / ∑ Math switches what you're writing. Switching while you write splits off a new block at the cursor. In a Math block, Enter finishes the formula and goes back to text.

    Math syntax (AsciiMath, or LaTeX if it starts with a backslash):

    x^2 + y_1
    (a+b)/(c-d)
    sqrt(2) root(3)(x)
    int_0^oo e^(-x^2) dx
    sum_(n=1)^N 1/n^2
    lim_(x->0) sin x / x
    (del n)/(del T)
    alpha, Delta, lambda, pi
    a <= b, x != y, +-, xx, ~~
    [[a,b],[c,d]]
    vec v, hat x, bar z, dot x

    In text, wrap math in $…$, or just write it — things like E = mc^2 or x_1 + x_2 are spotted automatically.

    Lists: start a line with - , 1. , # (heading) or [ ] (checkbox). **bold** works too.

    ✨ Clean up fixes spacing, capitals, doubled words and spelling, and leaves math alone. Words it doesn't know can be added to your dictionary. In ink areas it smooths your strokes. Undo (↶) reverts it.

    S Pen: in a text block, write with the pen to type (Samsung's S Pen to text, or the handwriting mode of the Samsung Keyboard). In an Ink area, draw freely; hold the pen's side button to erase.
